# BoomBoom on Android: how to install it

The app is one file, **BoomBoom.apk**. Someone sent it to you, or you can download the newest one yourself from
<https://github.com/tohin003/BoomBoom/releases> (under **Assets**). It takes two minutes.

**You need:** an Android phone with Android 8.1 or newer (any phone from about 2018 on).

## Install

1. **Get the file onto your phone.** If it came by WhatsApp, Telegram, e-mail or Google Drive, or you downloaded it
   in the phone's browser, it is already there.
2. **Tap the file.** The first time, Android says that this app (WhatsApp, Files, Chrome: whichever you tapped it
   in) is not allowed to install apps. Tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, go **back**, and tap
   **Install**.
3. **If Google Play Protect asks**, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. It asks because BoomBoom does not
   come from the Play Store, so Google has never seen it. (If it offers to scan the app first, that is fine: let it.)
4. **Open BoomBoom.** Pick a song.
5. When the first song starts, Android asks whether BoomBoom may show notifications. Tap **Allow**: that is the
   player on your lock screen and in the notification shade, with pause and skip.

That is all. Music keeps playing with the screen off and while you use other apps.

## Signing in (optional)

Without signing in you can search and play anything. To get your own playlists, likes and recommendations, tap the
round icon at the top right, then **Sign in**, and sign in with your Google account.

- The sign-in is Google's own page; BoomBoom never sees your password.
- If Google says it cannot sign you in there, tap **Trouble signing in?** at the top of that page and try again: it
  shows Google the page in a second way.
- You can add up to three accounts and switch between them in Settings.

## If something is not right

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "BoomBoom can't start yet" | Tap **Open the Play Store** and update **Android System WebView** (a part of Android that BoomBoom plays through). Then open BoomBoom again. |
| "App not installed" when installing | Your phone is older than Android 8.1, or it is out of space. |
| Music stops after a while with the screen off | Some phones (Xiaomi, Redmi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus, some Samsung) put apps to sleep to save battery. Open the phone's **Settings > Apps > BoomBoom > Battery** and choose **Unrestricted** (or "No restrictions", "Don't optimise"). |
| A song will not load | Check the connection and tap the song again. The first song after installing can take a few seconds longer than later ones. |
| Anything else | In BoomBoom tap the round icon at the top right, then **Settings**, scroll to the end and tap **Report a problem**. Send the file it makes to whoever gave you the app. It holds what the app did (times, song ids, formats), your phone's model and Android version, and nothing personal. |

## Updating and removing

- **A new version** arrives as a new BoomBoom.apk. Install it the same way, over the old one: your settings,
  downloads and sign-in stay.
- **To remove it**, press and hold the BoomBoom icon and choose **Uninstall**. Everything it kept goes with it.

## Good to know

- BoomBoom is a personal project, not a Google or YouTube product.
- Downloads are kept inside the app and play without a connection. They do not appear in your phone's music folder.
- The equaliser and amplifier ("Sound", from the player or from Settings) change the sound of BoomBoom only.
- Settings (the round icon at the top right) also has the sound presets, the song cache and the downloads switch.
