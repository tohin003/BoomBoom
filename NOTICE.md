# Notices

## Head-related impulse responses (the Spatial mode)

`Packages/BoomEngine/Sources/CBoomAudio/BoomHRIR.c` is made by `Tools/HRIR/make_hrir.py` from:

> Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, "HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone", MIT Media Lab Perceptual
> Computing, Technical Report #280, 1994. https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html

The data is copyright 1994 by the MIT Media Laboratory and is provided free with no restrictions on use, provided
the authors are cited when the data is used in any research or commercial application. The diffuse-field equalised
set is used, the horizontal plane only, resampled from 44.1 kHz to 48 kHz.

## Filters

The equaliser's filter forms are those of Robert Bristow-Johnson's "Audio EQ Cookbook".
